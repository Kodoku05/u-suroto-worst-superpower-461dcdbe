## Selection

Winner: #12. It names a concrete, genuinely useful power, then turns it into the exact failure the reader recognizes: security achieved by locking out the owner. It earns the joke immediately and uses fewer words than the alternatives.

Runner-up: #11. The mind-reading loop is more conceptually novel, but it is less clearly the worst power on first read because the loop still reveals a real thought. #12 wins at criterion 1: its limitation makes the promised function actively useless to its user.

Second-pass refinement: changed “changing it to” to “making it,” reducing the winner from 93 to 88 code points while keeping the reversal direct.
